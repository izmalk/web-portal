## Summary

I'm a technical writer and documentation engineer with over 15 years of experience across academia, large enterprises and technology startups. I create developer documentation for complex products and build automation that improves quality, strengthens consistency and makes documentation workflows more efficient. My priorities are user success and a positive user experience: helping users accomplish their goals with confidence and minimal friction.

My experience covers cloud platforms (OpenStack, AWS and GCP), NoSQL databases (Tarantool, TypeDB, OpenSearch), SaaS products and security certification processes. I use a docs-as-code workflow based on Git and pull requests, collaborating closely with architects, engineers, DevOps and SRE teams.

I've led cross-functional documentation teams of up to 15 people, including technical writers, analysts and DocOps specialists. I've mentored writers and built workflows that connect documentation with product strategy. I treat documentation as part of the product experience, using feedback and testing to keep improving it.

My portfolio includes product guides, tutorials and references; terms of reference, technical specifications and feasibility studies; acceptance and test documentation; technical and financial bids; knowledge base articles and scientific publications.

I value long-term collaboration and the opportunity to grow with a team and its products. I'm based in the UK on a Skilled Worker visa and require visa sponsorship.

### TypeDB (ex Vaticle): Senior Technical Writer

### VK (ex Mail.Ru Group): Documentation Lead

I joined Mail.Ru Group, later renamed VK, in 2019 as a Senior Technical Writer on the Tarantool team. I documented an open-source, in-memory transactional database with a built-in Lua application server, writing in English and translating the documentation into Russian.

In 2020, I became Team Leader for the VK Private Cloud documentation team. I recruited and trained a cross-functional team and developed my skills in people management, project management and agile working practices.

In 2022, my responsibilities expanded to a second documentation team, covering both Private Cloud and Public Cloud products.

### Research and early career

My career began in 2008 while I was studying at [Bauman Moscow State Technical University](https://bmstu.ru/). I worked part-time as a technician and software developer, documenting educational projects and events and supporting IT systems.

After graduation, I joined the [All-Russian Research Institute for Civil Defense and Emergencies](https://vniigochs.ru/) for postgraduate study and later, work. Over eight years at the institute, I progressed from junior researcher to a head of research department for automated systems, helping establish my own department after completing my PhD.

Research strengthened my documentation skills, attention to detail and understanding of the software development lifecycle. I found technical communication especially rewarding because it brought together my research background, technical knowledge and interest in explaining complex systems.
