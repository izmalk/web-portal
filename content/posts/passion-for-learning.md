My deepest passion is exploring new technologies, products and user experiences. I'm eager to learn, whether I'm working with a new framework, investigating a hardware architecture or trying a web interface.

<!--more-->

I value the close connection between my profession and my interests. My work lets me keep learning new technologies while helping other people.

I started learning programming languages at school, but I always wanted to explore further: Pascal, Windows administration, hardware and networks.

My first job was a part-time internship as a junior software developer while I was at university. That experience taught me three things:

1. Learning and exploring are fun.
2. Repeatedly using the same technologies to solve similar problems can become monotonous.
3. My mind was starting to work more like a compiler. The more time I spent thinking about code and debugging, the more I noticed changes in my thought processes.

I decided to explore other IT roles where I could keep learning and use a broader range of skills.

Since then, I've worked as a system administrator, analyst, QA specialist, researcher, technical writer and team leader. The last two roles have suited me best.
