My deepest passion is learning new technologies, products, and user experiences. I am eager to learn, whether it's a new framework, new hardware architecture, or a new web interface.

<!--more-->

I am truly happy that my profession is so deeply connected with my hobby. I love doing my job as it lets me keep learning new technologies and helping other people.

I started learning programming languages in school. But I always looked for something more to learn — not only Pascal, but also Windows OS administration, hardware, and networks.

My very first job was as an intern junior software developer. It was a part-time job while studying at university. And while working, I understood three things:

1. It's kinda fun learning and exploring.
2. It's kinda boring just using the same technologies over and over again to solve similar tasks.
3. My mind was changing to something more like a compiler. The more I was thinking about the code and debugging, the more I noticed changes in my thought processes.

So I decided that I didn't want to be a full-time software developer. Instead, I would look for some other IT-related profession.

Since then, I have tried being a system administrator, analyst, QA, researcher, technical writer, and team leader — with the last two being the most suitable professions for me.
